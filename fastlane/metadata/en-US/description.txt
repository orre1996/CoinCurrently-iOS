CoinCurrently is the private, anonymous way to track crypto. No sign-up. No accounts. No ads. No personal data. Just a fast, beautiful crypto tracker built for everyone.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Your data stays on your device. We don’t collect personal information, we don’t sell anything, and we don’t share your activity with advertisers. There are no ad networks, no advertising IDs, and no third-party trackers watching what you look at.

• No sign-up — open the app and start using it
• No account, no email, no phone number
• No ads, ever
• No advertising IDs, no cross-app tracking
• No personal data collected
• Wallets, transactions, and settings are stored locally on your device
• Biometric lock (Face ID / Touch ID) to keep your app private

The only analytics we use is Aptabase — a privacy-first, open-source tool that records anonymous, aggregated events (like “app opened” or “chart viewed”) so we can fix bugs and improve the app. No user IDs, no IP addresses, no personal data — ever.

ACCESSIBILITY FOR EVERYONE

Crypto should be for everyone. CoinCurrently has full accessibility support across the entire app.

• VoiceOver — every screen is labeled and fully navigable, from Home and Market to Portfolio, News, and Settings
• Dynamic Type — text scales with your system settings for easier reading
• Color-blind mode — switch price changes from green/red to blue/orange in Settings
• High-contrast, clean UI in both light and dark mode
